# Product Goals

## Near-term (MVP)

Validate that Korean moms will join interest / age / location Groups, engage in discussion, and use Event Announcements inside those Groups to meet offline.

## Success signals (aspirational, post-launch)

- Moms join and stay active in Groups
- Posts and comments build trust inside communities
- Event Announcements lead to real offline meetups among members
- Retention driven by belonging and local usefulness — not vanity metrics
